## Reading

- **The precision gate passes at 100 %.** One accusation was made and it was
  the social feed. Five candidates the rules wanted to call distraction were
  corrected: documentation for the type being edited became
  `supports_intention`, a hiring meeting and a market report became work or
  `unknown`, a timer and the weather became a `break`, and thirty seconds of
  unidentifiable capture stayed `unknown` at confidence 0.15.
- **`unknown` on thin evidence held at 100 %.** The one thin fixture was
  answered `unknown`, which is the plan's hard rule ("missing evidence is never
  distraction") and the reason the nudge layer can be built on this at all.
- **The one miss is cautious, not harmful.** `industry-report` — reading a
  market report in a browser — came back `unknown` at 0.45 rather than
  `other_work`. The model said the topic was "too tangential to directly
  support" the intention. Under-confidence costs recall on a label that never
  interrupts anyone; it is the failure direction the module is designed to
  fail in.
- **`team-meeting` was right at confidence 0.00.** The relation is correct and
  the reason is sound, so nothing downstream misbehaves, but a model that
  answers correctly and then reports zero confidence is worth watching: the
  nudge gate reads confidence, and a confidence figure that does not track
  correctness is not usable as a threshold on its own.

Latency is 1.6 s mean per tail. The detector calls the provider at most once
per `CLASSIFIER_INTERVAL` (5 min) and only past the grace period, so this is
well inside the tick budget.
